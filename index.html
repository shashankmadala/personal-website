The long version.

I'm Shashank Madala, a student at Robbinsville High School in Robbinsville, New Jersey, class of 2027. Almost everything I work on ends up in the same place: machine learning, pointed at the parts of human behavior that are hard to measure.

Kora started with my autistic cousin. I founded it and built it for him, leading a team of three developers: an AI app that reads emotional cues and gives caregivers something they can act on. It supports 700+ users across 25 therapy centers in 12 countries, and it won the 2025 Congressional App Challenge for New Jersey's 10th district, announced by the office of U.S. Representative LaMonica McIver.

Lumin AI is the 501(c)(3) nonprofit I founded and now run as co-CEO. It makes AI education free: a 20-module curriculum, completion certificates, and chapters run by students. 15,000+ students taught, 5,200+ certificates issued, 50+ chapter locations across 16+ countries, and a 125+ intern team behind it.

At the Yale Child Study Center I am a research intern in the Cha Lab, modeling autism-related social communication from real parent and child interaction video, audio and pose data. My first-author paper is accepted at IEEE MIT URTC 2026. It looks at a released model that scores autism severity from a child's speech. Summarize the same recording twice and the score moves by a standard deviation of 0.385 ADOS-2 points, 0.325 from reordering alone. An order-invariant model with 365x fewer parameters removes the part that comes from order. I present it at MIT on October 10, 2026, and it is to be published in IEEE Xplore after the conference.

The rest, briefly. I represented the USA at the 2026 International Economics Olympiad in Shenzhen, where Team USA placed 6th of 52 countries in the business case competition. I am president of the Robbinsville FBLA chapter and spent two years on the New Jersey FBLA State Technology Committee. I write software for FRC Team 2590 Nemesis, Curie Division winners at the 2024 FIRST World Championship. Along the way: USACO Gold Division and the Presidential Volunteer Service Award at the Gold level twice.

Coverage has run in Trenton Journal, NJBIZ, India-West, New Jersey Stage, The Global Indian, TheUNN, Lokvani, TAPinto and CanvasRebel, plus the Yale School of Medicine profile and the announcement from Rep. McIver's office. Every piece is linked below.

03 RESEARCH INTERN · FIRST AUTHOR

Yale Child Study Center

  • 120+HOURS OF DATA
  • 40FAMILIES
  • 0.80AUC, ANXIETY RISK
  • 365xSMALLER MODEL, URTC PAPER

In the Cha Lab, I build AI that models autism-related social communication differences from real parent and child interactions. The work spans 120+ hours of video, audio, and pose data from 40 families: a child-vs-adult detection module, synchrony scoring, and fusion models reaching AUC 0.80 for predicting anxiety risk in autism. It is the same question behind Kora, asked with lab data instead of an app.

My first-author paper, accepted at IEEE MIT URTC 2026, takes apart a released open-source model that scores autism severity from a child's speech. Two choices are made arbitrarily before the model sees anything: which groups of vocalizations are drawn, and what order they are listed in. Summarizing one recording a second time moves the child's score by a standard deviation of 0.385 ADOS-2 points, 0.325 from reordering alone. An order-invariant model with 3,266 parameters, against 1,190,785 in the released network, removes the ordering part entirely, at an accuracy difference this sample cannot resolve. I present it at MIT on October 10, 2026. It is to be published in IEEE Xplore after the conference.
