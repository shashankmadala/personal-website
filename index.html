ABOUT

The long version.

I'm Shashank Madala, a student at Robbinsville High School in Robbinsville, New Jersey, class of 2027. Almost everything I work on ends up in the same place: machine learning, pointed at the parts of human behavior that are hard to measure.

Kora came out of volunteering with families of autistic kids. It is an AI app that reads emotional cues and gives caregivers something they can act on, built with a four-student team and inspired by my cousin. It supports 700+ users across 25 therapy centers in 12 countries, and it won the 2025 Congressional App Challenge for New Jersey's 10th district, announced by the office of U.S. Representative LaMonica McIver.

Lumin AI is the 501(c)(3) nonprofit I founded and now run as co-CEO. It makes AI education free: a 20-module curriculum, completion certificates, and chapters run by students. 15,000+ students taught, 5,200+ certificates issued, 50+ chapter locations across 16+ countries, and a 125+ intern team behind it.

At the Yale Child Study Center I am a research intern in the Cha Lab, modeling autism-related social communication from real parent and child interaction video, audio and pose data. First-author work is in preparation for MIT URTC 2026.

The rest, briefly. I represented the USA at the 2026 International Economics Olympiad in Shenzhen, where Team USA placed 6th of 52 countries in the business case competition. I am president of the Robbinsville FBLA chapter and spent two years on the New Jersey FBLA State Technology Committee. I write software for FRC Team 2590 Nemesis, Curie Division winners at the 2024 FIRST World Championship. Along the way: USAMO qualifier, USACO Gold Division, and the Presidential Volunteer Service Award at the Gold level twice.

Coverage has run in Trenton Journal, India-West, New Jersey Stage, The Global Indian, TheUNN, Lokvani, TAPinto and CanvasRebel, plus the Yale School of Medicine profile and the announcement from Rep. McIver's office. Every piece is linked below.

03 RESEARCH INTERN

Yale Child Study Center

In the Cha Lab, I build AI that models autism-related social communication differences from real parent and child interactions. The work spans 120+ hours of video, audio, and pose data from 40 families: a child-vs-adult detection module, synchrony scoring, and fusion models reaching AUC 0.80 for predicting anxiety risk in autism. It is the research version of the same question behind Kora: can a machine help you understand what someone is feeling? First-author work in prep for MIT URTC 2026.

  • Multimodal ML: vision, audio, pose
  • Fusion models reaching AUC 0.80
  • Two manuscripts in progress

A low-cost pedestrian detection system that retrofits older cars: a Raspberry Pi and Coral TPU running a TensorFlow Lite model at 95% real-time detection accuracy.
