01 CO-CREATOR

Kora

An AI app that helps caregivers of autistic children read emotional cues, built with a four-student team and inspired by my cousin. It started from a pattern we kept seeing while volunteering with families: caregivers misreading emotional signals. It now supports 700+ users across 25 therapy centers in 12 countries.

  • Winner, 2025 Congressional App Challenge, NJ-10
  • Headed to the U.S. Capitol winner showcase, 2026
  • Y Combinator Startup School 2026
  • CNN-based emotion modeling
  • Seed funding from a UPenn innovation group

03 RESEARCH INTERN

Yale Child Study Center

In the Cha Lab, I build AI that models autism-related social communication differences from real parent and child interactions. The work spans 120+ hours of video, audio, and pose data from 40 families: a child-vs-adult detection module, synchrony scoring, and fusion models reaching AUC 0.80 for predicting anxiety risk in autism. It is the research version of the same question behind Kora: can a machine help you understand what someone is feeling? First-author work in prep for MIT URTC 2026.

  • Multimodal ML: vision, audio, pose
  • Fusion models reaching AUC 0.80
  • Two manuscripts in progress
Yale School of Medicine profile
04 CO-FOUNDER

SafeStrides

A low-cost pedestrian detection system that retrofits older cars: a Raspberry Pi and Coral TPU running a TensorFlow Lite model at 95% real-time detection accuracy. Piloted across 200 student vehicles in partnership with school administration and the Robbinsville Police Department, then iterated through two national competition cycles.

  • Blue Ocean: Top 250 of 23,000+ (2026)
  • Blue Ocean: Top 350 of 12,784 (2025)
  • Diamond Challenge: 2nd in New Jersey
05 SOFTWARE LEAD

FRC Team 2590, Nemesis

Third year writing software for one of FIRST's most competitive robotics teams: I built the team's first PnP pose estimation using fiducial IDs, its first object detection pipeline, and autonomous routines in Java and WPILib, while mentoring newer programmers. In 2024 we won the Curie Division at the FIRST World Championship and reached the Einstein semifinals.

  • 2024 World Championship, Curie Division winners
