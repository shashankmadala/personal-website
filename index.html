03 RESEARCH INTERN

Yale Child Study Center

In the Cha Lab, I build AI that models autism-related social communication differences from real parent and child interactions. The work spans 120+ hours of video, audio, and pose data from 40 families: a child-vs-adult detection module, synchrony scoring, and fusion models reaching AUC 0.80 for predicting anxiety risk in autism. It is the research version of the same question behind Kora: can a machine help you understand what someone is feeling? First-author work in prep for MIT URTC 2026.

  • Multimodal ML: vision, audio, pose
  • Fusion models reaching AUC 0.80
  • Two manuscripts in progress

04 CO-FOUNDER

SafeStrides

A low-cost pedestrian detection system that retrofits older cars: a Raspberry Pi and Coral TPU running a TensorFlow Lite model at 95% real-time detection accuracy. Piloted across 200 student vehicles in partnership with school administration and the Robbinsville Police Department, then iterated through two national competition cycles.

  • Blue Ocean: Top 250 of 23,000+ (2026)
  • Blue Ocean: Top 350 of 12,784 (2025)
